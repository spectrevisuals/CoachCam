CoachCam 1.8.5

  • Reliable with external cameras & mics. If a USB camera or microphone disconnects or drops mid-recording, CoachCam now notices within a few seconds, stops cleanly, and saves everything filmed up to that point — both video and audio — with a clear message. No more lost check-ins from a flaky cable.
  • No frozen face. If the camera drops, its picture-in-picture is removed straight away instead of freezing on the last frame.
  • Plug and play. Cameras and microphones now show up the instant you connect them — no need to quit and reopen the app.
  • Clearer error reporting behind the scenes, so any future hiccup is much easier to diagnose.
