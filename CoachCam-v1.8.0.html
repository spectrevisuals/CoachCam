CoachCam 1.8.0

  • New: countdown sounds. A gentle 3-2-1 tick that resolves into a chime when recording starts, and a soft chime when it stops. Toggle sound on/off any time.
  • New: skip or turn off the countdown. Start recording instantly, or tap start now during the 3-2-1.
  • New: float cam redesign. A clean orange branded ring, a small CoachCam badge, and a softer rounded backdrop.
  • Fixed: mirror now applies to the saved video, not just the live preview.
  • Fixed: custom area no longer brings the CoachCam window in front of what you're recording — the screen you're boxing stays visible (click to cancel).
  • Fixed: stopping a recording returns you to the record screen so sending to WhatsApp is quicker.
  • Fixed: the app icon now shows in the Dock.
