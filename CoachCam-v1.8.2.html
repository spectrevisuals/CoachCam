CoachCam 1.8.2

  • Long recordings fixed. Lengthy check-ins no longer end up as broken, unplayable or 0-byte files. The app now stays awake for the whole recording (no background throttling while minimised), and if a capture is ever interrupted it saves what it can and tells you clearly instead of failing silently.
  • Auto-hide now defaults off. The before/after screen stays on view while you record over the float cam — and there's a new auto-hide toggle right there so you can switch it on when you want to talk over Google Sheets etc.
  • WhatsApp photos refresh automatically. New check-in photos appear without quitting and reopening the app — switch back to CoachCam (or hit the new refresh button) and they're there.
  • Client list matches WhatsApp. The before/after client picker is now ordered by your most recent chat, just like WhatsApp's own list.
