CoachCam 1.8.9

  • Never lose a check-in to a silent capture glitch. On rare occasions macOS could stop feeding the screen to CoachCam without any error, leaving a recording empty. CoachCam now spots this within seconds, stops, and tells you to restart — instead of filming a whole check-in that turns out blank. If it happens partway through, everything recorded up to that point is saved.
  • Straight-line drawing tool. When annotating photos or drawing on screen during a recording, switch between freehand and a straight line (or just hold Shift) — handy for showing bar paths and alignment.
  • Faster fixes. Recording problems now report more detail automatically, so issues get diagnosed and fixed quicker.
