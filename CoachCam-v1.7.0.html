CoachCam 1.7.0

  • Custom area recording — drag a box and record just that part of your screen, Loom-style. A live orange outline shows exactly what's in frame while you record (and stays out of the saved video).
  • Auto-hide — optionally hide the CoachCam window the moment recording starts, so you can talk over Google Sheets, Photos, anything.
  • The window now jumps back to the front when you stop, so sending the check-in to WhatsApp is one click.
  • Resize the float cam by dragging it to any size (remembered next time), and set the custom area straight from the float cam too.
  • Recordings now sort into weekly "WC" (week commencing) folders, and are named after the client you picked in the WhatsApp tool — easier to find.
