CoachCam 1.9.0

  • 28-day free trial. New coaches now get full access to CoachCam free for 28 days — record check-ins, compare photos side by side, pull from WhatsApp, all of it. Existing subscriptions are unaffected.
  • Never lose a check-in to a silent capture glitch. If macOS ever stops feeding the screen to CoachCam, it now catches it within seconds and saves what was recorded, instead of losing the check-in.
  • Straight-line drawing tool. When annotating photos or drawing on screen, switch between freehand and a straight line (or just hold Shift) — handy for bar paths and alignment.
