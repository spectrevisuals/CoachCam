CoachCam 1.8.1

  • Smarter pose matching. Smart match now compares body shape (cropped to the person, pose-aware, front/back/side aware) instead of raw pixels — far better pairing of before/after photos.
  • Before/after opens on Smart match by default, with modes ordered smart match · by date · paste.
  • Easier manual line-up. Clear on-screen hint: A / D moves the left photo, ← / → moves the right. (Toggle “reverse a+d and arrows” to swap.)
  • Float cam drops into your custom area automatically (bottom-right) so the face-cam sits inside what you’re recording.
  • Auto-hide is on by default, and the option label is no longer cut off.
