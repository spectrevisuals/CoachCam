CoachCam 1.9.4

  • Edit your recordings. Fluffed a bit mid-check-in? Hit edit on the saved popup — mark the section, cut it (as many cuts as you need), preview with the cuts skipped, and save an edited copy. Your original is always kept.
  • Find where you said it. In the editor, type a word or phrase into find in recording and jump straight to every place you said it. The first search transcribes the clip on your Mac in seconds — nothing is ever uploaded.
  • Auto-backup. Pick a backup folder in the new ⚙ settings and every recording is copied there automatically. Point it at iCloud Drive, Google Drive or Dropbox and your check-ins upload themselves — your storage, kept as long as you need.
  • Send a link instead of a file. Backing up to Dropbox? Hit show backup on the saved popup, right-click the file → Copy Dropbox Link, and paste a streaming link into any check-in app.
  • Your branding. Upload your logo and pick your colours in ⚙ settings — they appear on your recordings, the float cam (ring + logo badge, with its own ring colour option), and exported before/after comparisons. Everything your clients receive carries your brand.
  • Sturdier than ever. This update ships with a new automated test suite covering recording, device-failure recovery, editing maths and photo exports — plus anonymous usage stats (never client names, files or transcripts) to help us improve CoachCam.
