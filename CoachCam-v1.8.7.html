CoachCam 1.8.7

  • Draw on screen. While recording, tap the pencil (in the float cam or the recorder) and drag to draw lines over anything — a paused check-in video, a form cue — to show where a movement should go. Tap again (or press Esc) to stop, and the eraser to clear.
  • Zoom any photo. In every before/after view: double-click a photo to zoom in, pinch to zoom smoothly, drag to pan. Zoom resets when you move to the next photo.
  • Same-day check-ins. Clients who check in more than once a day (e.g. show prep) now show as separate, time-stamped entries in the date picker instead of one lumped-together day.
  • Duplicate photos fixed for good. WhatsApp's SD + HD copies of the same photo collapse reliably now — without ever hiding two genuinely different poses.
