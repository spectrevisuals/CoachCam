CoachCam 1.8.4

  • WhatsApp audio fixed. Recordings now play with sound when sent on WhatsApp (voice and screen audio are saved as one track).
  • Recordings can't be lost. Long check-ins save reliably. If a recording is ever interrupted, CoachCam now stops and tells you straight away — and keeps the part already filmed as a normal clip — instead of failing at the end.
  • Clearer errors. If a save ever fails, you get the actual reason (and it's written to a log file) rather than a generic message.
  • Client search. A search box on the before/after screen to quickly find any client.
  • Smoother before/after. Search and photo scrubbing (A/D + arrows) now work together without getting in each other's way.
