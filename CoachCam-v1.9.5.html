CoachCam 1.9.5

  • New: computer sound on/off. Reviewing a client's training video that's got loud gym music? Turn computer sound off in the recorder bar and your check-in records just your voice — the video still plays out loud for you, the music never reaches your client.
  • New: automatic weekly transcripts. Flip on save transcripts automatically in ⚙ settings and every check-in's transcript is added to one weekly document next to your recordings (and in your backup folder) — by Friday you've got the whole week's check-ins in a single file to hand to your notes or an AI. Transcribed on your Mac, as always.
  • Copy the whole transcript. New copy transcript button on the saved popup and in the editor — everything you said in the check-in as text on your clipboard, with timestamps. Paste it into your notes, or into an AI for an instant client summary. Transcribed on your Mac; audio never leaves it.
  • Fixed: app frozen at launch on some Macs. A timing bug could freeze CoachCam on the way up (spinner, then "not responding") — most often on macOS 15. Gone for good.
  • Fixed: crash when picking a client on the newest macOS. macOS 27's new AI writing cursor could crash the app from the client search box. CoachCam now keeps that macOS feature out of its fields entirely.
  • Fixed: black video in webcam-only mode. Recording just your face (webcam only) with the float cam open could save a black video. Your camera now always fills the whole recording, edge to edge, with square corners.
  • Sturdier WhatsApp client list. If your Mac has a leftover second copy of WhatsApp's data (e.g. after switching to WhatsApp Business), CoachCam now always reads the one with your real chats — and if the list is genuinely empty, it tells you why instead of showing a blank picker.
  • Automatic crash reports. If CoachCam crashes or a recording doesn't save properly, it now sends us a short technical report (error codes, timings, which kind of camera and mic) so we can fix it quickly — often before you notice. Never client names, recordings, file names or transcripts.
