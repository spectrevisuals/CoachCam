CoachCam 1.9.5

  • Copy the whole transcript. New copy transcript button on the saved popup and in the editor — everything you said in the check-in as text on your clipboard, with timestamps. Paste it into your notes, or into an AI for an instant client summary. Transcribed on your Mac; audio never leaves it.
  • Fixed: app frozen at launch on some Macs. A timing bug could freeze CoachCam on the way up (spinner, then "not responding") — most often on macOS 15. Gone for good.
  • Fixed: crash when picking a client on the newest macOS. macOS 27's new AI writing cursor could crash the app from the client search box. CoachCam now keeps that macOS feature out of its fields entirely.
  • Sturdier WhatsApp client list. If your Mac has a leftover second copy of WhatsApp's data (e.g. after switching to WhatsApp Business), CoachCam now always reads the one with your real chats — and if the list is genuinely empty, it tells you why instead of showing a blank picker.
