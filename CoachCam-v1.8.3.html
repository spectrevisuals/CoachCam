CoachCam 1.8.3

  • Recordings now play in WhatsApp. Voice and on-screen sound are merged into a single audio track when the clip is saved, so audio no longer goes silent after sending via WhatsApp (it was fine on Mac/iPhone but WhatsApp only played one of the two tracks).
  • AI match stays put. Switching to WhatsApp and back no longer clears your before/after pairing — returning to CoachCam keeps the match in place.
  • Smarter auto-hide. Now remembered separately per screen: the recorder defaults to hiding the window (handy for talking over Google Sheets), while the before/after screen keeps it on view so your photos stay visible. Each has its own toggle.
