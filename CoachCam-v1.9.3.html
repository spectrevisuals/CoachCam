CoachCam 1.9.3

  • Audio fixed for good. Found and fixed the real cause of recordings where audio played too fast or cut off early — a timing bug that silently discarded part of the microphone audio on some Macs. Every recording now keeps every bit of your voice, in sync, on every mic.
  • Records the right monitor. CoachCam now records the screen its window is on by default — move the window and it follows. Pick a different monitor from the display menu any time and your choice sticks.
  • Draw mode polished. A floating toolbar appears while you're drawing so you can switch pen/line, clear, stop drawing, or stop the recording — no more getting stuck. Also fixed the app appearing to zoom while drawing.
  • Fix AI pose matches by hand. In smart match, hit ai match wrong? to line up the correct after photo, then save match to lock it. Step poses with the arrow keys, or use manual scroll to browse each side freely.
  • Sort a mixed batch. Client sent before and after photos in one message? sort before / after lets you tag each photo — and leave the food and sleep screenshots out of the comparison.
  • Clearer photo tools. The WhatsApp strip now shows always-visible before / after buttons under each photo, and clear all resets your poses for the next check-in.
  • Built-in guide. New ? button (and a one-time welcome tour) covering recording, float cam, drawing, photo tools, and one-click buttons to fix screen-recording and WhatsApp permissions.
  • Send to WhatsApp is more reliable, including after switching to a WhatsApp Business account.
