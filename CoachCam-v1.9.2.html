CoachCam 1.9.2

  • Audio stays in sync. Fixed a rare issue on some Macs where a recording's audio could play back too fast. CoachCam now records at your microphone's real sample rate, so voice and video line up.
  • Smoother client search. Searching for a client and switching between clients is snappier — no more slowdown or spinning wheel after a lot of searching.
  • Tidier app icon. Removed a white box that could appear around the CoachCam icon in the Dock.
